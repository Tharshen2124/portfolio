Hackerspace MMU is a community where people with shared interests in coding, tech, art and more meet up and build things. Most of us are self-taught, but we lean on each other for guidance, especially when starting something new. I joined in June 2022 and was President from September 2023 to September 2024.

## What I did

- **Onboarded members** and helped them come up with their own projects to work on
- **Managed sessions**, where members give IdeaTalks and ProgressTalks on what they're building
- **Coordinated the design** of the new Hackerspace logo
- Reached **high numbers of member registrations**
- **Upheld the principles** of Hackerspace MMU

## Mentorship

I owe a lot to Willie Poh, who guided me throughout my presidency. What he taught me made me a better programmer, a better leader and a better person.

## Still here

I've stayed involved since stepping down. I later led the rebuild of **Hacktrack**, the 15-year-old system Hackerspace uses to track members, projects and meetups. We added a new API layer, a Next.js committee dashboard, an automated registration pipeline and a Discord bot, all running at zero cost. It's the first thing I've deployed that real users rely on.
