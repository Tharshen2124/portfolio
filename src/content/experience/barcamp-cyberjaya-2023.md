I led the **Technical Department** for **BarCamp Cyberjaya 2023**, a participant-driven unconference focused on open discussion and knowledge sharing. BarCamp has no pre-set schedule or speakers. Attendees bring and vote on the topics, which makes it informal and collaborative.

The following year I became Event Director and led [BarCamp Cyberjaya 2024](/experience/barcamp-cyberjaya-2024).
